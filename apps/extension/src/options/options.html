OKauto Assistant settings

Pairing

In your OKauto dashboard open Settings → Extension, create a pairing token, and paste it here. The token is stored locally and sent only to your API URL.

How it works

  1. Open the extension popup and pick a vehicle from your queue.
  2. We open Marketplace and fill the form for you — you stay in control.
  3. You review and click Publish. We never submit forms for you.
  4. The listing goes LIVE in your dashboard automatically.
